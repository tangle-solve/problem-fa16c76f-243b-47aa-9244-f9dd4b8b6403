### BCGT-000421: Personhood credentials resistant to rental
- **Subfield:** Governance, pseudonymous participation and research public goods
- **Open:** Characterize privacy and anti-collusion guarantees when unique-person credentials can be rented, delegated or coerced without key transfer.
- **Status:** Research target beyond proof-of-personhood constructions dating to 2017.
